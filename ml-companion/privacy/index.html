ML Companion privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026

ML Companion ("the app") is an unofficial companion app for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, made by Muiz Fahmi, publishing as Lonepan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and the services it uses collect, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other personal details.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to deliver game data and to understand crashes and usage. These Google services collect some device and usage information, described below.

Information we collect ourselves

None. The app downloads game data (heroes, items, statistics, patch notes) from our database; it does not upload anything you enter or do in the app to us.

Information collected by services the app uses

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app shows a small number of ads. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalised using this information.

Google Firebase

  • Cloud Firestore delivers the app's game data. Like any server, it processes your IP address and basic request information to respond.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects usage information such as app opens, screens used, session length, app version, device model, operating system, country, and an app instance ID and advertising ID. We use it in aggregate to see how the app is used and improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports: the state of the app and device when a crash happens, and a crash installation ID. We use it only to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring collects performance data such as app start time and network request timings, along with device and app information, to help us keep the app fast.

These services are provided by Google, which processes the information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Firebase's data practices are also described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your choices

  • Ad consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it any time in the app under More → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection by it.

Data retention and security

Information collected by Google services is kept and protected according to Google's own policies and retention settings. Analytics data is kept for the standard Firebase retention period. Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.
